# How can I remove the date at the top of the template

## Customer
How can I remove the date at the top of the template? I don't want the date there. Thanks,

## Customer
I figured out how to take the date off but couldn't figure out how to not have a lot of spaces on top of paper before the wording starts.

## Support
Hi Brenda,

By default, the letter includes space for the sender's information, recipient's address, date, and subject line. This structure is what creates the space at the top of the page.

If you would prefer to remove these elements and start your text higher up, you can enable the "Text Only" option in the letter settings.
