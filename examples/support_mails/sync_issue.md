## Support
Hi Ahmed

Thank you very much for your message. I'll be happy to help you get synchronization working again. Please check the following requirements for smooth synchronization:

1. You are signed in with the same Apple ID on both devices.
2. iCloud is enabled on both devices.
3. The Letters app is allowed to store data in iCloud on both devices (see also https://support.apple.com/en-us/118225).
4. The latest iOS version is installed on both devices.
5. The latest version of the Letters app is installed on both devices.

If these suggestions do not resolve the issue, I kindly ask you to provide me with more details about your devices, such as the hardware version and the iOS version.
