# New phone (transfer subscription)

## Customer
Hi, I paid a subscription through my iPhone, can I switch it over to my Android Galaxy S25?

## Support
Hi,

Thank you for reaching out with your question regarding transferring your subscription.

Unfortunately, it is not possible to directly transfer a subscription initiated through your iPhone (via the Apple App Store) to an Android device (like the Samsung Galaxy S25). This is because Apple manages all in-app subscriptions and payments on the iOS platform, and they do not currently offer a mechanism for transferring these subscriptions to a non-Apple operating system like Android.

Furthermore, please note that while we do have an Android version of the app, it is currently a free version and does not yet support all the features available in the iOS version.

We apologize for any inconvenience this may cause and appreciate your understanding.
