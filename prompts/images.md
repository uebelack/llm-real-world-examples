Core Aesthetic: 2D flat vector illustration style, clean digital line work, no heavy gradients or 3D shading. Mild humorous tech comic book aesthetic. Clear, bold typography for speech bubbles.

Color Palette: Muted corporate tech palette. Dominant colors are slate blues, grays, and deep sage greens. Exactly one vibrant, glowing amber/orange accent color reserved strictly for AI-related elements (like the LLM brain or glowing wires).

Character Sheets:

The Developer: Male, friendly face with a short brown beard and messy brown hair. Always wears a dark teal zip-up hoodie over a gray-and-white striped t-shirt, blue jeans, and brown sneakers. Frequently holds a gray ceramic coffee mug with "404" printed on it.

The CEOs: A panel of expressive, slightly cartoonish business professionals in suits (one man with glasses, one woman with long brown hair in a pink shirt, one man in a dark suit with a red tie).

The AI Agent: A small, friendly brass/gold retro-styled robot with simple glowing eyes and a rectangular torso.

Environment & Background: A clean, organized software developer's workbench or lab. Elements include green metal filing cabinets, oscilloscopes, tools, and computer monitors displaying green code or "Passed" test results.

Composition Constraint: Maintain a highly scannable, clean layout with ample whitespace so the graphics work perfectly on presentation slides.
